PaperIn turns a photo of a document into a clean, shareable PDF in a few seconds.

Point the camera at the page: the document is detected automatically, the
perspective is corrected and the image is enhanced. No manual cropping, no
adjustments, no watermark.

Features
* Automatic document detection and perspective correction
* One-tap capture, import from the gallery, reorder and rotate pages
* Color, grayscale and black & white filters
* Export to PDF or JPEG, save or share directly
* Text recognition (OCR) in multiple languages, fully offline
* Blue-on-white interface, light and dark theme

Privacy
* All processing happens on your device
* No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics
* The app does not even request the INTERNET permission: it works in airplane mode
* Minimal permissions: camera, and storage only for saving files on older Android versions
